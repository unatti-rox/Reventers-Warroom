# Project-type adaptation

Don't apply one editing formula universally. Pick the closest type (or blend two) and let its priorities shape the selects, the three contact-sheet directions and the QC emphasis.

## Real estate
Architecture · property identity · lifestyle · location · amenities · landscape · developer identity · approved factual claims · regulatory information (e.g. registration numbers) · disclaimer · CTA · accurate representation.
Never invent amenities, views, distances, infrastructure, specifications or regulatory claims — and never let AI-generated visuals imply them. Renders vs. actual-site footage must be distinguishable where regulation requires it ("artist's impression").

## Product
Product visibility · geometry (no distortion, no wrong variants) · features · benefits · demonstration · product and brand identity · CTA.

## Corporate
Message clarity · interviews · B-roll that supports what's said · brand consistency · titles/lower-thirds · data and graphics (only approved numbers) · professional pacing · speech intelligibility.

## Interview / testimonial
Speaker credibility and intelligibility first · cut on meaning, not just pauses · B-roll covers jump cuts honestly (don't change what was said) · names/titles verified · captions edited, not raw.

## Social reel
First-frame impact · strong first 1–3 s · immediate context · retention · mobile readability · platform safe zones · pattern changes · clear payoff · CTA. Often viewed muted — the story should survive without sound.

## Cinematic brand film
Visual storytelling · emotional progression · cinematography · controlled pacing · composition · sound design and music · premium typography · purposeful transitions. Longer shots are allowed to breathe.

## Performance ad
Hook · problem/benefit · product or offer clarity · proof · retention · CTA · version testing (alt hooks / CTAs) · message speed. Offers and prices must come from approved material only.

## Launch film / event film
Launch: build anticipation → reveal → key messages → CTA/date. Event: atmosphere, scale, key moments, people, sponsors as contracted; chronology optional but geography should read clearly.

## Multi-format adaptation
Re-frame per aspect ratio rather than letterboxing by default; re-check safe zones, text scale and crop of faces/products/logos for each format. Each new format is a deliverable that still sits under the approved direction — but if the adaptation changes structure (e.g. a 6 s cutdown from a 60 s film), present lightweight contact-sheet options for it.
